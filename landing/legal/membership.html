What is included with Membership

  • Unlimited reading of all published interactive 3D pop-up storybooks on tales.gurulao.com.
  • Clear English read-aloud narration with word-by-word highlighting and subtitles in English, Lao, Thai, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.
  • Read Mode and Auto Read built for young English learners.
  • Every new storybook added to the library as it publishes.
  • One parent-owned account shared across the website and upcoming iOS and Android apps.
  • No ads, no third-party tracking, and a parent gate before account sign-in or billing.

Cancel any time

You can cancel your membership at any time with no cancellation fee. When you cancel, your family keeps full access until the end of your current paid billing period, after which your account simply returns to the Free plan (with Kapi and the Crocodile still free).

Standalone Storybook Editions: Illustrated e-book editions sold separately on Apple Books, Google Play Books, Amazon KDP, and Kobo are one-time purchases managed by each store and are separate from tales.gurulao.com membership.
