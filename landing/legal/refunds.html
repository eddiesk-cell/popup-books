Refund & Cancellation Policy

Try Kapi and the Crocodile free before you subscribe. When paid membership opens, cancellations and refunds stay clear and simple.

1. Cancel any time

You can cancel your monthly or yearly membership at any time before your next billing date. Cancellation stops future charges immediately, and your family keeps reading access until the end of the current paid period.

2. Web membership refunds

For memberships purchased on tales.gurulao.com, we offer a 14-day refund on your first membership purchase if it is not the right fit for your family, as well as prompt refunds for accidental renewals or unresolved technical issues. To request a refund, email support@tales.gurulao.com with your account email and order receipt.

3. Apple App Store & Google Play subscriptions

Memberships purchased inside our iOS or Android apps are billed directly by Apple or Google:

  • Apple App Store: Manage or cancel in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Request refunds through Apple's support portal (reportaproblem.apple.com).
  • Google Play: Manage or cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Request refunds through Google Play's order history.

4. Processing time

Approved web refunds are issued back to your original payment method; depending on your bank or card issuer, funds typically appear within 5–10 business days.

1. ຍົກເລີກໄດ້ທຸກເວລາ

2. ການຄືນເງິນສຳລັບການສະໝັກຜ່ານເວັບໄຊ

3. ການສະໝັກຜ່ານ Apple App Store ແລະ Google Play

ສະມາຊິກທີ່ຊື້ໃນແອັບ iOS ຫຼື Android ຈະຖືກຮຽກເກັບເງິນໂດຍກົງຈາກ Apple ຫຼື Google:

  • Apple App Store: ຈັດການ ຫຼື ຍົກເລີກໃນ ການຕັ້ງຄ່າ iOS → ບັນຊີ Apple → ການສະໝັກສະມາຊິກ. ຂໍຄືນເງິນຜ່ານໜ້າຊ່ວຍເຫຼືອຂອງ Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).
