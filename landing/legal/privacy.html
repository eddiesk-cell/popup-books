Privacy Policy

Built for families with children ages 4–8. Sign-up is for the parent only, with minimal data, no ads, and no tracking.

Our Children's Privacy Promise: We never show ads, never include third-party tracking or social SDKs (such as Facebook), never sell or share data, and never knowingly collect personal data directly from children.

1. Sign-up is for the parent

Every account on tales.gurulao.com is created and owned by a parent or legal guardian behind a parent gate. Children can read stories without entering any personal information.

2. Minimal data we collect

  • Parent account info: your email address and sign-in provider identifier (Google, Apple, or email sign-in link).
  • Membership status: whether your account is Free, Active, or Cancelled, the billing source (web, Apple, or Google), and the end date of the current paid period.
  • Language preference: saved locally on your device so stories open in your chosen subtitle language.
  • Waitlist email (optional): only if you choose to leave your email for launch notification.

We do not collect child names, dates of birth, photos, voice recordings, contacts, or precise location.

3. No ads and no tracking

There are no advertisements, no behavioral analytics, no third-party ad pixels, and no cross-site tracking cookies anywhere on Wonder of Saya Tales. Fonts and book assets are self-hosted.

4. Trusted service providers

To keep accounts secure and deliver books reliably, we use limited infrastructure providers strictly as data processors: Supabase (parent authentication and membership database), Cloudflare / Hostinger (hosting and book file delivery), and—when paid membership opens—our authorized payment provider, Apple App Store, or Google Play. We never see or store your full payment card number.

5. Data retention and deletion on request

We keep your parent account data only while your account is active. You may request complete deletion of your account and associated email records at any time by emailing support@tales.gurulao.com from your account email address. We confirm and complete all deletion requests within 7 business days.

6. Questions or parents' rights

If you believe a child has submitted personal information without parental consent, or if you want a copy or deletion of your account data, please contact us immediately at support@tales.gurulao.com.
